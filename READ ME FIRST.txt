WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
Paste a YouTube link.  Golem's Brief Deck listens to the video and gives
you back ONE of two things — your choice:

  (1)  RESEARCH REPORT  -- a markdown briefing (.md file)
       Structured sections, verbatim quotes, tables, and a custom
       "Your Angle" section that ties the source back to YOUR
       situation.  Great for memos, pre-meeting prep, due-diligence.

  (2)  BRIEFING DECK    -- a PowerPoint (.pptx file)
       Navy + gold corporate slides, 8-22 of them.  Great for
       putting in front of a Plant Manager / CEO / investor.

You pick the mode with a radio button in the app.  Takes about
30-60 seconds per output.


HOW TO USE IT  (3 STEPS)
------------------------

STEP 1.  DOUBLE-CLICK:    "Start Golem's Brief Deck.bat"
         A black window will open.  This is normal.
         Wait 5-10 seconds.  Your browser will open by itself.

STEP 2.  IN THE BROWSER:
           - Look at the LEFT SIDEBAR
           - Paste an API key (Gemini "AIza..." is FREE -- recommended)
           - Click "Save"
         (First time only.  After this, the key is remembered.)

STEP 3.  IN THE MAIN AREA:
           - Pick a mode:  RESEARCH REPORT  or  BRIEFING DECK
           - Paste a YouTube URL
           - Optional: add a topic to focus the AI
           - Optional: add YOUR angle to get a custom section
           - Click the "Generate" button
           - When it's ready, click "Download"


WHEN YOU'RE DONE
----------------
Close the black console window.  That stops Golem's Brief Deck.
Open your downloaded .pptx file in PowerPoint.  Done.


FIRST TIME?  YOU NEED AN API KEY  (3 OPTIONS)
---------------------------------------------
Golem's Brief Deck auto-detects which provider you're using.
For most people, Option A (Gemini) is the easiest.


** OPTION A  -  GOOGLE GEMINI  **  RECOMMENDED  **
==================================================
FREE.  No credit card required.  1,500 decks per day on free tier.

1.  Go to:  https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
2.  Sign in with your Google account
3.  Click "Create API key"
4.  Copy the key (starts with "AIza")
5.  Paste it into Golem's Brief Deck's sidebar
        (it auto-saves once you click Save)

That's it.  60 seconds.  Zero cost.


OPTION B  -  OPENAI  (cheaper than Anthropic, ~2-5 cents per deck)
1.  Go to:  https://platform.openai.com/api-keys
2.  Sign up + add payment method
3.  Add a few dollars of credit
4.  Click "Create new secret key"
5.  Copy the key (starts with "sk-proj-" or "sk-")
6.  Paste it into Golem's Brief Deck's sidebar


OPTION C  -  ANTHROPIC  (best quality for technical / industrial content)
1.  Go to:  https://console.anthropic.com/
2.  Sign up + add payment method
3.  Add a few dollars of credit (a deck costs about 5-15 cents)
4.  Visit "API Keys" --> "Create Key"
5.  Copy the key (starts with "sk-ant-")
6.  Paste it into Golem's Brief Deck's sidebar

SOMETHING NOT WORKING?
----------------------
- The black window closes immediately?
    Python may not be installed.  Install Python 3.11 from
    python.org first, then double-click "Start Golem's Brief Deck.bat" again.

- "Module not found" error in the black window?
    Open a terminal in this folder and run:
        pip install -r requirements.txt
    Then try again.

- The browser doesn't open?
    Look at the black window for a line like
    "Network URL:  http://localhost:8501"
    Open that URL in your browser manually.

- Anthropic says my key is invalid?
    Double-check you copied the whole thing (starts with sk-ant-).
    Make sure you added credits at console.anthropic.com.
